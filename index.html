1. Introduction

Welcome to Salah Tracker ("we," "our," or "us"). Salah Tracker is designed to help Muslims stay consistent with their five daily prayers through habit streaks, real-time prayer calculations, and optional friend accountability.

We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, collect, and use, as well as your rights regarding that information when using our mobile application (iOS & Android).

2. Location Information & Usage

To accurately compute daily Islamic Prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha), Salah Tracker requires access to your device's geographical location.

🔒 Local Location Processing: Your precise GPS coordinates (Latitude and Longitude) are used exclusively on your device to calculate accurate astronomical prayer times using standard mathematical algorithms (Adhan). Your raw location is never sold, uploaded, or transmitted to remote advertising or tracking servers.

Permissions Used:

  • Foreground Location Access (GPS): Used to fetch current latitude and longitude when opening the app or refreshing prayer times.
  • Reverse Geocoding: Converts coordinates into a city and country name to display on your local app dashboard.

3. User Account & Data Storage

Salah Tracker offers both local standalone features and cloud-assisted social tracking features:

  • Local Device Storage: Your daily prayer logs (Prayed, Delayed, Missed, Excused), streaks, points, and application preferences are stored locally on your device using encrypted key-value storage (`AsyncStorage`).
  • Optional Cloud Account (Supabase): If you choose to sign up with Google or Email to connect with friends, we store minimal account information (such as your account ID, display name, and prayer status counts) in our backend cloud database (`Supabase`) to sync status updates with your connected friends.

4. Notifications & Reminders

If permitted, Salah Tracker delivers local push notifications to alert you when prayer times begin or when reminder countdowns complete.

Notifications are scheduled locally by your device OS. You may disable notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We respect your privacy and do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to third parties, ad networks, or data brokers. We do not include third-party advertising SDKs.

We use the following trusted infrastructure services:

  • Expo SDK: Core framework for application runtime and location access.
  • Supabase: Secure cloud database and authentication provider for friend sync.
  • Adhan JS Library: Open-source, client-side mathematical prayer calculation engine.

6. Children's Privacy

Salah Tracker is designed for general audiences and is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Local data can be cleared at any time by clearing the application storage or uninstalling the Salah Tracker app from your device.

If you created an online account, you may request complete account and data deletion at any time by contacting us through the support email below.
