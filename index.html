This policy explains how Salah Tracker handles information when you use the mobile app, visit this policy page, or contact us. It describes the app's current features. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to qrmate00@gmail.com.

The essentials

  • Prayer tracking works without a Google login.
  • Prayer records are saved on your phone. The current app does not upload or sync them to our database.
  • Google sign-in creates an app account and shows your profile. It does not back up your prayer records.
  • Deleting your Salah Tracker account keeps the prayer records on your phone and leaves your Google account intact.

1. Information we use

Prayer records and preferences

The app stores the prayer dates, prayer names, statuses you choose, and Qaza completion information you record. It also saves your prayer-calculation preferences and your selected location, including its coordinates and place label. These are used to calculate prayer times, remember your choices, and display your history and progress.

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides authentication information to create or access your Salah Tracker account through Supabase. This can include your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile picture. Supabase assigns an app account identifier and manages authentication. Your phone saves account/session information so you can stay signed in.

Your profile picture may be loaded from the picture address provided by Google. Salah Tracker does not receive your Google password.

Location

When you request location detection, the app asks for foreground location permission and uses the phone's location services to obtain coordinates. It may use the phone's geocoding service to turn those coordinates into a place name. The operating system and its location/geocoding providers may process location and request information to provide these services.

The app saves the chosen location on your phone and calculates prayer times on the device. The current app does not upload your saved coordinates to our Supabase database or use background location tracking. You can manage location permission in your phone's system settings. Refusing permission limits location-dependent prayer-time features.

Service and support information

Google and Supabase may process technical and security information needed to provide authentication, such as connection information and records of sign-in, sign-out and account-related events. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can answer your question or handle your request.

2. How local prayer records work

Prayer records and preferences are stored separately from your Google/Supabase account. They are shared across guest use and different app logins on the same phone. Signing out, switching accounts, or deleting an app account does not erase those records.

Anyone who can open the app on that phone may be able to see its local prayer history. The current release does not provide account-specific prayer storage, cloud sync or an app-operated cloud backup of prayer records.

To erase local records and preferences, use your phone's app-storage controls to clear Salah Tracker's app data. This also resets the app and signs you out on that phone; it does not delete your server-side app account. Device or operating-system backups may retain or restore app data according to your backup settings. Deleting an app account cannot remotely erase data or backups held on your devices.

3. Use and sharing

We use the information described above to operate the app, provide optional account access, calculate and display prayer times, show your progress, secure authentication, and respond to support or deletion requests.

We do not sell your personal information. The current app does not include advertising or an app-usage analytics SDK. Information is processed by service providers as needed for the features you use, including:

  • Google: account sign-in, profile information, and relevant device services. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: app authentication, account information and related server functions. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Your device's operating system and location providers: permission, location, geocoding and any device-managed backup services you use.

Providers may process information in countries where their services operate. We may also disclose information when legally required, to address misuse or security threats, or when you ask us to do so.

4. Retention and security

Local prayer records and preferences remain on your phone until you clear the app's data or they are otherwise removed by your device. App account information is retained while your account exists. Account deletion removes the active Salah Tracker authentication account and its associated profile identity information.

Authentication and security logs, provider backups, and support correspondence may be retained where needed for service operation, security, resolving requests or legal obligations, subject to the relevant provider's retention practices. An account-deletion request does not remove information independently held by Google as part of your Google account.

The app uses HTTPS for its communications with Supabase. Device data is stored in the app's local storage under the operating system's protections. No storage or transmission method is completely secure; protect access to your phone and Google account.

5. Delete your Salah Tracker account

You can delete your app account from the app or request deletion by email without reinstalling or opening the app.

In the app

  1. Sign in to the Salah Tracker account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation and select Delete account.

When deletion succeeds, the app removes your Supabase authentication account and signs you out on that phone. If deletion fails, the app displays an error so you can retry or contact support.

Request deletion without the app

Email qrmate00@gmail.com with the subject Salah Tracker account deletion request. Send the request from the Google email address used to sign in, if you can, and tell us you want your Salah Tracker account deleted.

Email an account deletion request

This button opens an email draft; it does not send the request automatically. You can also send the email directly using the address above.

We verify account ownership before processing an emailed request. We handle verified requests as soon as reasonably possible and confirm completion by email. If more information or additional time is needed, we explain this and the expected timing in our reply. Never send your Google password or one-time sign-in codes.

What is deleted, and what stays?

  • Deleted: your Salah Tracker authentication account and its associated active profile identity information, such as the name, email and profile picture reference stored with that account.
  • Kept on your phone: prayer records, Qaza history, saved location and calculation preferences. They are device-local and are not linked to the app account in the current release. Use your phone's app-storage controls if you also want to remove them.
  • Unaffected: your actual Google account and information independently held in Google's services.
  • Limited retention: security logs, provider backups and request correspondence may remain as described in section 4.

Signing in with Google after deletion can create a new Salah Tracker account. Existing local prayer records will still be available on the phone unless you erased them separately.
