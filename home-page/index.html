Review your recorded prayers across weekly, monthly and yearly periods. Prayer tracking works without creating an account.

Google sign-in is optional.

If you choose to sign in with Google, Bittu - Prayer Buddy uses your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile picture to create or access your app account through Supabase and show your profile.

The app does not receive your Google password. The current version does not sync or back up prayer records through your Google account.

Your records stay on your phone

Signing out or deleting your app account keeps your local prayer records. Account deletion removes the app account, leaving your Google account intact.
